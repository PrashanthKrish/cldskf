Demand-signal blindness in high-growth segments

SKF China currently has no systematic way to detect, qualify and route opportunity signals from Chinese-language sources — public tenders, provincial equipment-renewal programmes, OEM launches, robotics funding — into the sales pipeline at the moment they are actionable. This prototype ingests those signals, scores them for relevance, resolves them against known accounts, and gives Sales a weekly, rankable digest instead of relying on individual relationships and trade shows.
